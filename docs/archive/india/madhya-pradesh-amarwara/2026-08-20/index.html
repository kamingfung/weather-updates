⚠️ Limitations

  • Directional guidance only. This update should inform planning decisions but not substitute for on-the-ground observation or agronomic expertise.
  • External source dependency. ENSO intelligence relies on ENFEN, WMO, NOAA, and IRI bulletins, which publish on their own schedules (typically monthly). Between publication dates, conditions may have changed. Any source not updated within five weeks is flagged as stale.
  • Spatial resolution. Weather analyses cover a 25-100 km regional grid centered on Olmos, Lambayeque. Farm-level microclimates, topography, and irrigation effects are not captured.
  • Forecast accuracy degrades with time. Near-cast is reliable for days 1-3 (±1-2°C), moderate for days 4-10, and indicative only beyond day 10. The 6-month horizon should be used for strategic planning only.
  • About the forecast range. The seasonal forecast uses ECMWF SEAS5 with 50 ensemble members (via Open-Meteo). Each member starts from slightly different initial conditions to capture forecast uncertainty. The shaded band shows the 90% confidence interval across all members.
  • ENSO-weather link is probabilistic. El Niño development increases the probability of certain weather patterns in Peru but does not determine them.
  • No local validation or bias correction. Without an on-site weather station, systematic forecast errors cannot be detected or corrected. Installing one is recommended for operational decisions.
